#### WireGuard VPN

Added a WireGuard client for syncing to a home NAS while travelling, with
automatic connection when away from your home Wi-Fi. The WebUI supports
importing a WireGuard `.conf` file.

Thanks to @hiflyrf22 for the contribution.

#### SleepHQ

Faster uploads and more reliable recovery when a new therapy session
interrupts synchronization, avoiding unnecessary repeat uploads of
unchanged files. Fixed uploads being blocked unnecessarily by the
low-memory check.

#### Fixes and improvements

- Fixed report history and nightly statistics in STR.edf after changing
  the AirSense 11 / AirCurve 11 timezone.
- Reduced pauses during background work that could cause CAN receive errors.
- Automatic update checks now wait until post-therapy exports finish.
- Added SD card formatting and factory reset from the CLI.
