- **High-leak alerts**

  Enable a warning for sustained leaks, with an adjustable threshold and
  delay. On boards with audio output, including the Waveshare, alerts can
  sound through the speaker at a configurable volume.

- **Two-button shortcuts**

  Assign actions to a pair of buttons as well as individual buttons. New
  actions include power off, restart, sync, and disconnect from the therapy
  device. On Waveshare, holding BOOT + PLUS powers off on battery or enters
  sleep on USB power; PWR wakes it again.

- **Faster reports**

  Less waiting for detailed data when zooming, smoother navigation through
  the night, and charts that no longer jump around as data loads.

### Fixes and improvements

- **SleepHQ:** exports take priority over firmware update checks.
- **Export progress:** the EDF page now shows checks and sync activity that
  previously could run without a visible status change.
- **Report summaries:** pressure now uses IPAP, not mask pressure, and shows
  average, median, and 95th percentile.
- **Offline reports:** nights recorded in local EDF files remain discoverable
  and can have zoom data prepared without an AS11 connection.
- **Memory:** the web server and other noncritical buffers use less internal
  RAM, leaving more room for device communication and encrypted transfers.
- **Wi-Fi and BLE:** automatic Wi-Fi scans use Bluetooth coexistence support
  and reuse one scan across saved profiles during startup and recovery.
- **Crash diagnostics:** retained crash information is available after reboot,
  with compact log entries and an occurrence time when known.
- **CLI:** commands are grouped by purpose, status output is shorter, and
  configuration help shows descriptions and allowed values. Redundant
  commands and aliases have been removed.
