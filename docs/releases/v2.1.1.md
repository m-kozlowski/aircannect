### Important fixes

- **EDF capture**

  Recording no longer stops when a therapy mode does not provide every known
  stream signal. This particularly affects AirCurve 11 VAuto, which does not
  expose an ASV-only signal requested by AirCANnect.

- **EDF storage**

  Completed records are retained during temporary storage congestion instead
  of being dropped or shifting later data on the timeline.

- **Live oximetry**

  Local pulse and SpO2 readings appear in live charts when they are not being
  relayed through the AirSense or AirCurve connection.

- **Wi-Fi reconnects**

  AirCANnect avoids starting a second connection attempt while the Wi-Fi
  driver is already reconnecting.
