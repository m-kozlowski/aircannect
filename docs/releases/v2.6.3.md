#### Reports

Faster report generation with less SD activity and lower memory use.
Fixed rebuilding older and partially covered nights while preserving saved
sample coverage, and corrected session durations across daylight saving time
changes.

#### SPI SD

Fixed driver bugs causing write failures and read CRC errors at higher speeds.
SPI SD now defaults to 40 MHz, with automatic fallback to 20 or 10 MHz during
initialization. SDMMC defaults are unchanged.

#### Firmware updates

Fixed watchdog resets during OTA preparation and unnecessary upload lockouts
after an interrupted update attempt.
