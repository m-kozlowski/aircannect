### AirMini support

Added support for ResMed AirMini over CAN.

AirMini uses the same CAN wiring as AirSense 11. Keep the AirMini app closed
while recording: CAN and Bluetooth share the device's stream subscription.
AirMini Bluetooth connections.

### Fixes and improvements

- Fixed incorrect start and end times in some recorded sessions.
- Fixed screen orientation and auto-rotation settings not being saved.
