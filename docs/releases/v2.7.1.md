#### Optimized for limited memory

The main focus of this release is more reliable operation with limited
RAM, particularly on the Waveshare S3-GEEK with 2 MB PSRAM.

Loading history, building reports and opening the console now use much
less memory, reducing out-of-memory failures as your history grows and
leaving more room for other tasks. Period statistics also load faster,
with less repeated work and memory pressure when switching date ranges.

#### EDF and exports

Fixed statistics for sessions shorter than a minute and sessions crossing
noon, and corrected mask and other AS11 settings written to STR.edf for
OSCAR and SleepHQ.

#### ResMed firmware updates

AirCANnect now selects an available installation method and reports a
missing OTA key before uploading, avoiding a long transfer followed by a
timeout on stock BLE connections. Added support for preparing AS11
19.8.8.0 firmware images.
